About Stabilized Kerbol

  • Some orbits in stock Kerbal Space Program are known for their unrealistic properties. For example, Tylo would pull on Vall in a way that would either eject it, or crash it into another moon.
  • Playing around in Universe sandbox, I wanted to try and stabilize the Kerbolar system, to see how little changes I could get away with to stabilize the orbits.
  • When testing, only Vall, Minmus and Gilly needed adjustments.
