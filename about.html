About KrForge

Who I am

I'm KrForge. An individual software developer that hates where the internet has been heading: filled with AI, tracking, and general junk that no one wants. The software I write follows the philosophy of "Convenience without Surveillance". Provide a tool that makes your life a little easier, without collecting your data. The way all software should be.

What KrForge is

The name "KrForge" is new, but I have been working with software since 2016, when I was inspired by trying Scratch for the first time and since then, I have always loved to write code.
